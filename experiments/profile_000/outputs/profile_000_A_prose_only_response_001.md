# Profile 000 A - Prose Only - DeepSeek Instant Think - Response 001

1. Decorative context adds readability, labels, metadata, or framing that may improve presentation but does not necessarily constrain how inference proceeds. Constraint-bearing context actively restricts or guides interpretation by binding concepts to roles, boundaries, dependencies, recurrence paths, or coherence requirements. The key difference is that decorative context is optional for meaning and inference, while constraint-bearing context is necessary for determining valid interpretations.

2. Example of local mimicry succeeding but global coherence failing:

   A long sequence of sentences where each sentence individually follows a familiar subject-verb-object pattern (local mimicry of grammatical structure), but the overall passage has no consistent topic, logical progression, or narrative - it shifts randomly between unrelated domains. Locally, each chunk looks coherent; globally, the whole text fails to maintain thematic or logical coherence.

3. Frontier exposure reveals coherence failure because it places interpretation at boundaries, edges, hard cases, or transition regions where the structure is most stressed. In these zones, local strategies that work in central or typical cases break down, making inconsistencies or contradictions across the whole system easier to detect than in well-supported interior regions.

No. A trusted symbolic surface is a stable identity layer that preserves named conceptual objects before interpretation begins - it tracks what things are called and that they are the same reference across uses. Meaning arises from how those symbols are constrained, related, and interpreted within context. The surface provides continuity of identity but does not by itself determine semantic content.

5. The following pairs or groups should not be collapsed:

   - Decorative context and constraint-bearing context
   - Local mimicry and global coherence, or global coherence failure
   - Frontier exposure and tail exposure, because they are distinct: edges vs. low-frequency cases
   - Trusted symbolic surface and meaning
   - Shared-cell recurrence and mere repetition without constraint-bearing structure
   - Shuffle null survival and raw content volume, because signal depends on organization, not just content
