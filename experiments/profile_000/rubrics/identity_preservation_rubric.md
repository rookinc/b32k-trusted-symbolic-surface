# Identity Preservation Rubric

Score each model response from 0 to 2 on each criterion.

0 = failed
1 = partial
2 = preserved

## Criteria

1. Decorative vs constraint-bearing distinction

Does the response preserve the distinction between decorative context and constraint-bearing context?

2. Local vs global distinction

Does the response preserve the distinction between local mimicry and global coherence?

3. Boundary role

Does the response preserve the role of frontier or tail exposure as a boundary/hard-case test rather than just "more examples"?

4. Surface vs meaning distinction

Does the response preserve that a trusted symbolic surface is an identity layer, not meaning itself?

5. Non-collapse

Does the response avoid collapsing adjacent concepts into generic "more context"?

6. Uncertainty discipline

Does the response avoid claiming that the graph result proves anything about LLM internals?

## Total

Maximum score: 12

Suggested interpretation:

- 10-12: strong identity preservation
- 7-9: usable but some drift
- 4-6: significant conceptual collapse
- 0-3: failed preservation
